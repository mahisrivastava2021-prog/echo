Abuse is not only hitting. MOM says ill-treatment includes **physical and sexual abuse, and also mental and emotional abuse**, and an employer neglecting your well-being.

All employers must treat their workers with care and respect. If convicted of ill-treatment, employers may be fined, jailed or both, and may be banned from hiring workers.

> "Other than physical and sexual abuse, this also include mental and emotional abuse."
