Where you sleep must **protect you from sun, rain and strong winds**. You must be given at least **a mattress, a pillow and a blanket**, as well as bathroom amenities and toiletries.

> "The accommodation must adequately protect your MDW from environmental elements such as sun, rain or strong winds."
