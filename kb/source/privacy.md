You **must not be made to sleep in the same room as a male adult or teenager**. MOM says employers should give you a separate room if possible.

Cameras **must not be placed where they compromise your privacy or modesty**, for example where you sleep, change clothes, or in the bathroom.

> "Your MDW must not sleep in the same room as a male adult or teenager."
