If you are **in danger, call the Police at 999**.

If you are in distress but not in immediate danger, you can call MOM's **Migrant Domestic Worker Helpline at 1800 339 5505** (Monday to Friday, 8.30am to 5.30pm) to speak to an MOM officer. Mobile airtime charges may apply.

> "Call the Police at 999 if you are in danger."
