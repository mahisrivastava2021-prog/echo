You can ask for your salary to be paid **directly into your own bank account**, and your employer must do this if you request it. The account must be yours. It cannot be a joint account with your employer.

If you are paid in cash, there must be a **salary record that both you and your employer sign** each time. Keeping your own copy or photo of these records can help if there is a problem later.

> "You must pay your FDW via direct bank transfer if she requests for it."
