The salary you receive **must not be lower than the amount your employer declared to MOM** when applying for your Work Permit.

If you are being paid less than what was agreed and declared, you can ask MOM for help (see "How to report a problem to MOM").

> "The salary must not be lower than what you declared to MOM."
