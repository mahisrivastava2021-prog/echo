Your employer must pay your salary **every month**. It must be paid **no later than 7 days after the end of the salary period**. For example, salary for 1–31 May must be paid by 7 June.

A salary period cannot be longer than one month, so salary should not be held back and paid months later.

> "You must pay your FDW her salary every month, no later than 7 days after the last day of the salary period."
