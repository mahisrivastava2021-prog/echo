Your employer **must not keep your salary or any other money that belongs to you**, even if you ask them to keep it for you. They also must not keep your bank book or bank card.

This rule has applied since 1 January 2019. It protects you from losing access to your own money.

> "You must not keep your FDW's salary or any other money belonging to her, even if she requests for it."
