Your employer must give you **3 meals a day**, with enough food for the work you do (for example rice or bread, vegetables, and meat or fish).

Your employer should respect food you cannot eat because of your **religion or dietary needs**. Not being given enough food is a problem you can report to MOM (see "How to report a problem to MOM").

> "You must provide your MDW with 3 meals a day."
