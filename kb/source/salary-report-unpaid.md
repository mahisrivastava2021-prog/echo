If your salary has not been paid, you can contact MOM's **Migrant Domestic Worker Helpline at 1800 339 5505** (+65 6339 5505 from overseas), Monday to Friday, 8.30am to 5.30pm.

MOM can help you **recover your salary through conciliation**, a process where MOM helps you and your employer reach a solution. Your salary records and any messages about payment can help.

> "1800 339 5505 or +65 6339 5505 for overseas callers. (Operating hours: Mon – Fri, 8.30am to 5.30pm)"
