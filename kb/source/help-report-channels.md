There are several ways to report a problem like unpaid salary, overwork or not enough food:
1. **Report online** through MOM's website.
2. **Call the MOM Migrant Domestic Worker Helpline**: 1800 339 5505 (Mon–Fri, 8.30am–5.30pm).
3. **Call the Centre for Domestic Employees (CDE)**: 1800 2255 233, open 24 hours.
4. **Go in person** to the MOM Services Centre, Hall A, 1500 Bendemeer Road, Singapore 339946.

> "You can report the infringement online."
