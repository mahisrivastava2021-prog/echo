Your employer **should not keep your passport** and should not force you to give it to them. Your passport belongs to you (and your country's government), not to your employer.

A good employer gives you a safe place to keep it, such as a locker, and you must be able to reach it at any time. If your employer insists on holding your passport, you can contact MOM for help.

> "Employers should not keep the passports of their migrant workers or migrant domestic workers (MDWs). They should also not force their workers to give them their passport."
