MOM says your mobile phone is **your personal property** and you should have access to it. It is how you stay in touch with your family.

Your employer may agree **house rules with you** about *when* you use your phone, for example not while cooking, looking after children or giving medicine. Having rules about timing is normal. Being cut off from your phone completely is not what MOM describes.

> "A mobile phone is the personal property of your migrant domestic worker (MDW) and she should have access to it."
