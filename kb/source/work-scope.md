Under your Work Permit, you can **only do domestic (household) work**, and **only for your employer at the home address declared to MOM**. Your employer should not send you to work in their business or shop, or to clean other people's homes.

Your employer can be penalised for this, with a financial penalty of up to $10,000. (Looking after a child at a relative's home is only allowed in special cases that the employer must tell MOM about first.)

> "Can only work for her employer at the residential address declared to MOM."
