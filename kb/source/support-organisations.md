You do not have to handle this alone. Two organisations that help domestic workers:
- **Centre for Domestic Employees (CDE)**: a 24-hour toll-free helpline, 1800 2255 233, for help with employment problems.
- **HOME (Humanitarian Organisation for Migration Economics)**: a charity with a Domestic Worker Helpline at +65 9787 3122 (call or WhatsApp). See home.org.sg.

> "24-hour Helpline (Toll-free): 1800 2255 233 (1800-CALL-CDE)"
