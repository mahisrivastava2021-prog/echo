Every month, you must get **at least one rest day that cannot be "bought back"** with extra pay. Even if you agree to work on your other rest days, this one rest day a month must be a real day off.

If you could not take your rest day in a month, it can be moved to the next month (deferred by up to one month). Employers who do not give the mandatory rest day may face enforcement action from MOM.

> "You must ensure that your MDW has at least one rest day per month that cannot be compensated away."
