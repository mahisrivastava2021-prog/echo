To move directly to a new employer, the **new employer applies to MOM** and asks your **current employer to give consent** through MOM's online service. Both steps must be done at least 30 days before your Work Permit expires. When the new Work Permit is issued, the old one is cancelled.

This process depends on your current employer agreeing. If you are being mistreated and want to leave, talk to MOM, CDE or HOME about your options first.
