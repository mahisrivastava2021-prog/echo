Your employer must bear **all medical costs necessary for your health**. **You cannot be made to pay for your own medical expenses**, even the part above what the insurance pays.

> "You are not allowed to make your MDW pay for her medical expenses. This includes medical expenses that are part of, or above what the insurance pays."
