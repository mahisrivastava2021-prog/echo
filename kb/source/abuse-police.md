MOM takes abuse very seriously, **especially physical or sexual abuse**. Abuse cases are **investigated by the police**.

Employers convicted of abusing a domestic worker face severe penalties, and they and their spouses are **permanently banned** from employing another domestic worker.

> "If you suspect that an FDW is being abused or ill-treated, report the matter to the police. Complaints of abuse are investigated by the police."
